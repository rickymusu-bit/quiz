## Prompt esplicito esatto

Use case: stylized-concept.
Create ONE transparent PNG character animation key-pose sheet for Hellscape, a grim junkpunk gothic horror game set over a molten lava sea and black iron/stone bridge. Exactly TWO complete full-body views of the SAME original flying devil, arranged side by side in two equal generously padded cells: LEFT wings at the highest upstroke extreme, RIGHT wings at the lowest downstroke extreme. Both face RIGHT in a STRICT 90-degree SIDE PROFILE, orthographic camera, not frontal, not three-quarter. This is a new candidate design and a pair of flight extremes, not a finished animation.

Art direction: truly frightening adult dark fantasy horror, grotesque predatory bat-winged humanoid scavenger demon, emaciated sinewy dark oxblood flesh, charcoal leathery hide, angular skull-like face in profile, snarling jagged teeth, deep eye socket with a tiny ember glow, two swept backward bony horns, long clawed hands, digitigrade legs tucked naturally in flight, long thin barbed tail trailing left. Junkpunk is made of crude salvaged riveted black iron and oxidized bronze plates strapped sparsely to shoulder and forearm, rusted wire fastenings, one short worn chain fixed to the waist. Not a robot, no engine, no jetpack. Compact readable silhouette, terrifying feral anatomy, not cute, not chibi, not cartoon mascot, not a smiling red imp, not a muscular superhero, not a dragon. Exposed sinews and scarred texture, no gratuitous gore.

Style: richly textured hand-painted high-detail retro dark-fantasy game sprite, crisp controlled edges and clustered detail, consistent with a grotesque flesh-and-rusted-metal Cerberus against a gothic industrial lava bridge. Restrained palette of burgundy flesh, soot black, corroded iron, muted bronze, dirty bone; subtle warm orange reflected light from lava below, identical illumination in both poses. No neon colors, no bright candy red, no glossy clean armor.

Critical anatomical correspondence: SAME head, facial profile, horns, body, limbs, tail, armor, rivets, palette, scale and body position in both cells; only articulated wings and their membranes change pose. Two bat wings rooted to the upper back at anatomically fixed shoulders. Near wing clearly readable; far wing partly occluded with plausible depth, never extra limbs. Fixed lengths of upper wing bone, forearm and long supporting fingers across both poses. Same single bony wing-thumb claw attached to the wrist joint in BOTH poses; never move it to an elbow or to the wingtip. Wing membranes are scarred and slightly ragged with two small persistent tears on the SAME membrane panels in both poses. Raise and lower wings through their real shoulder/elbow/wrist joints: do not scale, stretch or redesign them. LEFT wing extends up above back with a plausible slight elbow bend, RIGHT wing sweeps down below torso while maintaining bone lengths; do not merely fold wings against the body. Both silhouettes must have recognizably different maximum wing heights. Keep the head/torso/pelvis at exactly the same scale, horizontal alignment and height in the two cells, tail pose and tucked legs unchanged. Slight side-view overlap is natural.

Composition: landscape sheet, equal cells, enough blank margin for raised and lowered wingtips, horns and tail. No cropping, no contact or overlap between the two figures. Genuine alpha transparency, including visible membrane tears; no floor, no scene, no lava, no cast shadow, no checkerboard drawn into the image, no labels, no lettering, no watermark. Exactly two poses, no third image, no intermediate poses, no grid lines.
